How it works

    Describe your subject

    Fill in the form below and copy the research prompt it builds for you.

  2. 2
    Ask any LLM

    Paste the prompt into Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or similar. A model with web search or a deep research mode gives the best results.

  3. 3
    Import, print or play

    Paste or upload the JSON reply here. Preview, edit and filter the cards, then print them on A4 with cut lines or test yourself with on screen challenges.
