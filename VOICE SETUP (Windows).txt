Flashcards: how to set up natural voices (VoiceStudio) on Windows

Voice mode can use VoiceStudio, a free app that gives the flashcards natural,
human-sounding voices and understands your spoken answers. Everything runs on
your own computer. Setting it up takes about 15 minutes the first time.

STEP 1. Download the flashcards
  1. Open this link. The download starts by itself:
     https://github.com/EstusHealth/learning/archive/refs/heads/claude/funny-shannon-ukgbkl.zip
  2. Open your Downloads folder, right-click the ZIP file and choose
     "Extract All...", then click "Extract".
     (Do not just double-click into the ZIP. Windows needs the files
     extracted before they can run.)

STEP 2. Start the flashcards
  1. In the extracted folder, double-click "Start Flashcards".
     - If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info",
       then "Run anyway".
  2. The first time, the Microsoft Store may open on a program called Python.
     Click "Get" or "Install", wait until it has finished, then double-click
     "Start Flashcards" again. You only need to do this once.
  3. A black window opens and the flashcards open in your browser.
     Leave the black window open while you play. Close it when you are done.

STEP 3. Install VoiceStudio
  1. Go to https://github.com/debpalash/VoiceStudio/releases/latest
  2. Under "Assets", click the file whose name ends in "win-x64.exe".
  3. Open the downloaded file to install it. If Windows says it protected
     your PC, click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  4. Open VoiceStudio. The first start can take a few minutes while it
     downloads its voice models.
  5. So it can understand speech: in VoiceStudio, open "Transcriptions" and
     download the recommended model if it offers one.

STEP 4. Turn on voice mode
  1. In the flashcards, load your deck (or click "Try sample"), then go to
     the "Play" tab.
  2. Tick "Voice mode" and set "Voice engine" to "VoiceStudio".
  3. A three-step checklist appears. Each step turns green by itself.
     When all three are green, pick a voice and press "Start".
  4. When the browser asks to use your microphone, click "Allow".

Every time after that: open VoiceStudio, then double-click "Start Flashcards".

If something goes wrong
  - The checklist says "You opened the file directly": close that tab and
    double-click "Start Flashcards" instead of "index.html".
  - It keeps saying "Waiting for VoiceStudio": make sure VoiceStudio is open
    and has finished starting. The page notices by itself within a few
    seconds.
  - It reads aloud but does not understand you: in VoiceStudio, open
    "Transcriptions" (or "Settings > Models > Dictation") and download the
    recommended model.
  - It hears itself and stops reading: turn off "Answer while it reads", or
    use headphones.
  - Nothing happens when you speak: check the microphone is allowed (the
    small icon at the left of the browser's address bar) and press "Mic".
